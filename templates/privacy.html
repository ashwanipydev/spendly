Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name and email address when you register.
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter manually.
  • Usage data: basic logs such as login times and feature usage, used solely to maintain the Service.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect exclusively to:

  • Provide and operate the Spendly expense tracking service.
  • Display your spending history, summaries, and category breakdowns.
  • Send account-related communications (e.g., password reset emails).
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve the reliability of the Service.

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it to any third party.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. We will process such requests within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may use a limited number of third-party services to operate (for example, a hosting provider). These providers are contractually required to handle your data only as directed by us and in accordance with applicable privacy law. We do not integrate with advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics platforms that track you across other websites.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided on our website.
